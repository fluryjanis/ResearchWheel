Everything you need to research

Useful research tools without the tab chaos.

Your research tools, one middle-click away

Select text, open Research Wheel, then search, save, translate, copy, or cite it without leaving the page.

Keep your research in one place

Save quotes, code, and sources as you browse so you don't lose important information across dozens of tabs.

Citations in seconds

Generate APA, MLA, Chicago, or BibTeX citations without formatting them yourself.

Search research sources faster

Send selected text to research tools such as Google Scholar and PubMed without manually opening another tab.

Private by design

Research Wheel runs locally on your computer. No tracking and no external servers.

Your research stays yours.

Research Wheel is designed around local-first research. Your workspace and research tools run locally rather than depending on a remote account or cloud service.

The project is open source, so you can inspect how it works for yourself.
